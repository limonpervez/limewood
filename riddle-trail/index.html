Hints that help

Most word games price hints so you give up and pay

The usual trick is to make the puzzle just hard enough, then price a hint just high enough, that eventually you crack and buy coins. The whole economy is built around your frustration.

Riddle Trail flips it. Solving earns more than hints cost, and three misses hand you a letter for free. You can play the entire trail without ever feeling squeezed.

Questions, answered

How do you play?

Read the riddle, then spell your answer from the letter tiles. Follow the trail through six worlds as you solve.

What if I get stuck?

You cannot stay stuck. Miss three times and the game gives you a letter for free, and solving always earns enough to afford hints.

Is everything unlocked?

You choose easy, medium or hard, and nothing is locked away. Play it the way you like.
