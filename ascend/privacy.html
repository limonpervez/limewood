Gym Coach — Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 14, 2026

Gym Coach ("the app") is published by Limewood. This policy explains what data the app handles.

The short version

Gym Coach does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. Everything you enter stays on your device.

What the app stores

To generate your workout plan and track your progress, the app stores the following locally on your device only:

  • Profile details you enter (age, sex, height, weight, fitness goal, experience level, equipment, schedule)
  • Your workout history (exercises, sets, reps, weights, dates)
  • Body weight entries you log

This data never leaves your device. The app has no account system, no server, and no analytics or advertising SDKs.

Data deletion

You can permanently delete all data at any time from Profile → Reset everything, or by uninstalling the app.

Children

The app is intended for users aged 13 and older.

Health disclaimer

Gym Coach provides general fitness information, not medical advice. Consult a qualified professional before starting an exercise program, especially if you have injuries or health conditions.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of the app adds online features, this policy will be updated before those features launch.
