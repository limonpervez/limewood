For the first day

Most fitness apps assume you already know how

Open a typical gym app and you are handed a library of exercises and told to build your own plan, as if you already knew a good session from a bad one. For a first-timer that is the hardest part.

Gym Coach starts one step earlier. It gives a complete beginner a plan for today, shows every movement as an animation, and walks them up five levels until training feels like second nature.

Good to know

Questions, answered

Is this good for a complete beginner?

Yes, that is exactly who it is for. It assumes no experience and shows every exercise as an animation so you always know what to do.

Do I need equipment?

The plan is built around standard beginner gym movements, each demonstrated so you can follow along at your own pace.

How does levelling work?

There are five levels. Keep completing sessions and the app moves you up, adding a little more as you get stronger.
