What it does

A word game that respects you

Everything here is built to help you relax, not to squeeze coins out of you.

No timers, ever

Play at your own pace. Nothing counts down, nothing rushes you, nothing punishes you for taking a break.

Zero ads

No banners, no pop-ups, no rewarded videos. There is no advertising code in the app at all.

Warm, tactile feedback

Found words light up in lantern colour with a satisfying settle. It is meant to feel good in the hand.

Handmade categories

Themed word lists to pick from, each hand-built so the puzzles stay fresh and coherent.

Free to play

The whole game is free. No coins to grind, no hints priced to make you give up and pay.

Made to unwind

A cozy corner of your phone for a quiet ten minutes, not another game demanding your attention.

Built the opposite way

Most word games are built to sell you hints

Open almost any word game and the design goal is obvious: make the puzzle just annoying enough that you buy a hint. Timers, coin counters, energy meters, ads between every level.

Word Search Cove has none of that. It is built to be the calm ten minutes at the end of your day, and nothing in it is designed to interrupt that or charge you for it.
