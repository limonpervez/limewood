Word Search Cove — Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 30, 2026

Word Search Cove ("the app") is published by Limewood. This policy explains what data the app handles.

The short version

Word Search Cove collects nothing about you. It has no account, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking of any kind.

The app is playable fully offline. It touches the internet for exactly one reason, described next.

The one time it uses the internet

On its first launch, the app downloads its display fonts (Fraunces and Nunito) from Google's public font service so the game looks the way it was designed. The fonts are then cached on your device and the app works offline from then on.

This request downloads font files only. It does not send any information about you, your device, or how you play. No personal data is transmitted at any point, and the app has no analytics or advertising SDKs that could do so.

What the app stores

So your progress is still there tomorrow, the app saves the following on your device only:

  • Your Endless Tides level and the furthest level you have reached
  • Which pack levels you have completed and the stamps in your Travel Log
  • Your Daily streak and streak-freezes
  • Your hint balance and lifetime stats (puzzles solved, words found)
  • Your chosen theme, and your sound and haptics settings

That is the entire list. There is no name, no email, no age, no location, no contacts, no device identifier, and no advertising ID. The app never asks who you are, because it does not need to know.

Puzzles are made on your phone

Every board is generated on your device from a number, not downloaded. The Daily puzzle is derived from the date, so every player worldwide gets the same board on the same day without a server being involved and without anyone being counted.

Children

Word Search Cove is suitable for all ages. Because it collects no data whatsoever, there is nothing for a child, or anyone else, to have exposed.

Data deletion

Everything lives on your device. Uninstall the app, or use Android's Settings → Apps → Word Search Cove → Storage → Clear data. Either way the data is gone. There is no copy anywhere else, because a copy was never made.

Ads and purchases

This version of Word Search Cove has no ads and no in-app purchases. Every category and pack is free. If a future version adds an optional paid upgrade or optional ads, this policy will be updated before that change ships, and any new data handling would be stated here plainly.

Changes to this policy

If a future version adds any new online feature, this page will be updated before that feature launches.
