What it does

Built to be forgotten — until it matters

Set your subscriptions once. Renewfox does the watching so you never get surprised by a charge again.

Smart reminders that fire

Get a nudge days before every renewal, engineered to actually arrive on time — not sit silently in a queue like other apps.

Trial Guard

Free trials get a louder, earlier warning before they quietly convert to paid — so a forgotten trial never becomes a surprise bill.

Scan a screenshot

Snap a receipt or confirmation email and on-device OCR fills in the name, price and date for you. Nothing leaves your phone.

Insights & forecasts

See exactly where your money goes each month, which category costs the most, and what next month is projected to run.

Renewal calendar

A month-at-a-glance calendar shows every upcoming charge and what each day of the month will cost you.

60+ presets, two-field entry

Popular services come pre-configured with their logo and colour. Adding one takes two fields — everything else is optional.

Private by design

Your money is nobody's business but yours

Most subscription trackers ask to link your bank, then quietly read every transaction you make. Renewfox is built the opposite way. It never connects to a bank, never asks for an account, and keeps every subscription you enter in a local database on your own phone.

There is no server for your data to sit on, because there is no server. Uninstall the app and it's gone — or export it yourself as a CSV any time.

Questions, answered

Does Renewfox connect to my bank?

No — never. You add subscriptions yourself (or scan a screenshot). Renewfox has no bank integration at all, so it can't see your balance, your transactions, or your money.

Do I need to create an account?

No. There's no sign-up and no login. Open the app and start adding subscriptions. Everything is stored locally on your device.

What's free and what's Pro?

The core tracker, reminders and Trial Guard are free. Pro unlocks extras like deeper insights — a one-time purchase, no recurring fee for a subscription app about subscriptions.

Is it on iPhone yet?

Renewfox is live on Google Play now. The iPhone and iPad version is in the works and will land on the App Store soon — join the waitlist and we'll let you know.

What happens to my data if I uninstall?

It's deleted with the app, because it only ever lived on your phone. You can export everything as a CSV first if you'd like to keep a copy.
