Stillwaters

A quiet verse, every morning · Android

A new Bible verse on your lock screen each morning, set once and then left alone. Most apps in this corner of the store make you search for a verse, save it, and apply it yourself, then charge five dollars a week for the privilege. Stillwaters does it for you, for a single small payment, and shows you no advertising at any point.

You never have to open it

Set it up once. The verse changes every morning on its own, forever.

No ads, not even when free

There is no advertising code in the app at all, so there is none to switch on later.

365 verses, a year without repeat

Every one checked word for word against the public-domain World English Bible.

Made for tired eyes

Warm near-black, never pure white on pure black. It is meant to be read at 5am.

Nothing that nags

No notifications, no streaks, no badges, no account. The app stays quiet.

Yours to share

Send any verse as a designed image, or keep it on your home screen as a widget.

Arrow Escape

A quiet arrow-maze puzzle · Android

Tap an arrow and it runs off the board along its own path, like a train on its own track. Only the straight road ahead of its head can stop it. Clear the board — but the arrows block each other in chains, so the order is the puzzle. No timer, no streaks, no energy meter waiting to run out.

One rule, and it is honest

Only the road in front of the head blocks an arrow. Nothing beside it counts.

Every board can be solved

Puzzles are built backwards from a solution, so a way through always exists.

Checking never costs you

Hold an arrow to trace where it would go. Only a real tap can spend a life.

A wrong tap teaches you

Miss, and the board shows you exactly which arrow stopped it.

Thousands of boards, no download

Every level is drawn on your phone from its own number. Nothing is fetched.

Paper, slate or dusk

Three hand-tuned themes, and a pinch to zoom when a board gets big.
