Safety comes first

A logbook, not a medical device

The app records information you enter and shows simple history and trends. It does not diagnose, calculate insulin doses, recommend treatment changes or replace advice from a qualified healthcare professional.

No dosing recommendationsNo emergency monitoringNo camera, location or contacts access
Private by default

Your health log stays with you

There is no account, advertising SDK or analytics tracker. Version 1.0 stores records, reminders, settings and an optional profile locally on your device.
