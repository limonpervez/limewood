Questions, answered

How does it set my lock screen?

Android has no lock-screen widget, so Daily Bible Verse sets your lock-screen wallpaper to a designed image of the day's verse, and refreshes it each morning.

Do I have to pay?

The app is free to try. A single small one-time payment unlocks it fully. There is no weekly or monthly fee.

Will it send me notifications?

No. There are no notifications, streaks or badges. It changes quietly in the background.
