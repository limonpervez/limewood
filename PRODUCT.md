# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Prospective users of Limewood's apps (first: Renewfox, an Android subscription tracker) who land here from Google Play, app store listings, or word of mouth. Secondary: Google Play reviewers verifying the developer site, and potential partners/press. Visitors are privacy-conscious consumers deciding in under a minute whether this studio feels trustworthy.

## Product Purpose

The official website of Limewood (limewood.app), an independent app studio. It exists to (1) make the studio look credible and human, (2) showcase Renewfox and drive Google Play installs when it launches, and (3) host the legal pages (privacy/terms/disclaimer) required by app stores. Success = a visitor trusts the studio enough to install the app or send a message.

## Brand Personality

Simple, Private, Honest. Calm confidence over hype — the site practices the same restraint the apps promise ("no dark patterns, no data harvesting, no bloat"). Warm and human, never corporate; crafted, never flashy.

## Anti-references

- Generic SaaS landing template: gradient hero, stat counters, cookie-cutter feature grids.
- Corporate agency site: sterile stock photos, buzzword copy.
- Overly cartoonish: the lemon/fox motifs stay refined, not childish.

## Design Principles

1. **Practice what you preach** — a privacy-first studio's site must itself be light, tracker-free, and honest (no fake numbers, no dark patterns).
2. **Show craft, not claims** — the phone mockup, icon work, and typography prove quality better than adjectives.
3. **One app at a time** — Renewfox is the hero; everything else supports it.
4. **Legible over decorative** — text contrast and readability win any tie against style.
5. **Fast on a phone** — the audience is Android users; the site must feel instant on mobile data.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.1 AA: 4.5:1 body-text contrast, visible focus states, reduced-motion alternatives for all animations, semantic landmarks, alt text on team photos. No auto-playing media.
